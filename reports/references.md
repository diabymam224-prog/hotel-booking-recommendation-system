## Dataset alternatives

- HotelRec: https://github.com/Diego999/HotelRec — very large review dataset; academic research use restriction and scale should be considered.
- 515K Hotel Reviews Data in Europe: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jiashenliu/515k-hotel-reviews-data-in-europe — useful for hotel review text, but the common schema lacks a stable reviewer ID for user-based collaborative filtering.
- Expedia Hotel Recommendations: https://www.kaggle.com/c/expedia-hotel-recommendations/data — user search/booking context and hotel-cluster targets; confirm whether its task and catalogue fit the research question before switching datasets.
